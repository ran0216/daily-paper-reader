## 摘要
现代边缘片上系统（SoC）集成了异构处理单元（PU），如CPU、GPU和NPU，它们各自具有不同的性能和能耗特征。在实时延迟和能耗约束下将AI推理工作负载部署到这些单元上，需要将工作负载联合映射到PU，并配置每个PU（例如，选择活跃核心数和运行频率）。这一联合空间呈组合式增长，使得穷举搜索不可行。大多数先前关于设计空间探索（DSE）的工作采用黑箱优化（BBO），如进化搜索，其中每次评估仅返回延迟和能耗等聚合指标。近期由LLM引导的DSE也依赖于同样稀疏的反馈。我们观察到，这限制了其效率：它无法提供对设计空间的洞察，也无法解释某一设计选择为何表现如此，并且使LLM的推理能力在很大程度上未被利用。我们提出TraceDSE，一种智能体式DSE流程，用于在异构SoC上为AI推理执行联合工作负载映射和PU配置选择。TraceDSE是一个由更丰富反馈（以系统执行轨迹的形式）驱动的迭代式提议者-评论者循环。LLM提议者智能体生成候选映射和PU配置以进行硬件评估。配备程序化轨迹分析工具的LLM评论者智能体分析这些轨迹，以识别瓶颈并提出有针对性的改进建议。该循环能够对每个设计点产生更深入的洞察、更高质量的决策以及更有效的搜索。在英特尔Meteor Lake SoC上的四个AI推理工作负载（不同复杂度的模型和一个多模型流水线）上，TraceDSE持续优于两种最先进的BBO工具，相比NSGA-II将帕累托前沿超体积最多提升35%，相比贝叶斯优化最多提升68%，同时所需的硬件评估次数减少约6-9倍。

## Abstract
Modern edge Systems-on-Chip (SoCs) integrate heterogeneous processing units (PUs) such as CPUs, GPUs, and NPUs, each with distinct performance and energy characteristics. Deploying AI inference workloads on them under real-time latency and energy constraints requires jointly mapping workloads to PUs and configuring each PU (e.g., selecting the number of active cores and the operating frequency). This joint space grows combinatorially, making exhaustive search infeasible. Most prior work on design space exploration (DSE) applies black-box optimization (BBO) such as evolutionary search, where each evaluation returns only aggregate metrics such as latency and energy. Recent LLM-guided DSE relies on the same sparse feedback. We observe that this limits its efficiency: it offers no insight into the design space or the reasons a design choice performs the way it does, and it leaves the reasoning abilities of LLMs largely unused. We present TraceDSE, an agentic DSE flow that performs joint workload mapping and PU configuration selection for AI inference on heterogeneous SoCs. TraceDSE is an iterative proposer-critic loop driven by richer feedback in the form of system execution traces. The LLM proposer agent generates candidate mappings and PU configurations for hardware evaluation. The LLM critic agent, equipped with programmatic trace-analysis tools, analyzes the traces to identify bottlenecks and suggest targeted refinements. This loop yields deeper insight into each design point, higher-quality decisions, and a more effective search. Across four AI inference workloads (models of varying complexity and a multi-model pipeline) on an Intel Meteor Lake SoC, TraceDSE consistently outperforms two state-of-the-art BBO tools, improving Pareto frontier hypervolume by up to 35% over NSGA-II and up to 68% over Bayesian optimization, while requiring ~6-9x fewer hardware evaluations.
