## 摘要
混合专家（MoE）模型通过稀疏激活扩展容量，但跨GPU推理会引入张量并行（TP）集合通信以及专家并行（EP）分发与合并操作。完成时间不仅取决于通信量，还取决于逻辑组如何映射到服务器内互连、GPU-NIC连接以及节点间网络。使用带有NS-3离散事件后端的ASTRA-sim，我们构建了一个包含32个GPU rank的受控矩阵，并将数据并行和流水线并行固定为1。工作负载是来自四种MoE配置的固定长度4096 token、类预填充的合成Chakra trace。实验涵盖六种服务器拓扑、四种TP/EP划分、两种TP集合通信算法和四种网络/拥塞控制模式，共产生768次确定性仿真。在每个模型启用反馈的144种配置子集中，暴露通信占平均完成时间的89.9%--95.8%。TP16EP2所需的平均完成时间是TP2EP16的3.68--4.35倍。在固定rank映射下，ASTRA-sim的双二叉树（DBT）比Ring多耗时28.3%--83.2%。类InfiniBand的高精度拥塞控制（HPCC）比基于RoCE的HPCC低约0.9%，而使用数据中心量化拥塞通知（DCQCN）的RoCE比RoCE HPCC慢23.8%--35.7%。拓扑效应是有条件的：Topology~6在低TP度时领先，但在高TP度时失去优势；额外的GPU或NIC只有在rank映射使流量在各注入路径间均衡时才有帮助。在均匀32路分片下，最大的检查点权重分片约为每个rank 48.75 GB，因此所有配置都满足每加速器64 GB权重驻留准则。在所评估的工作负载和模拟器语义下，服务器拓扑、并行性、集合通信实现和拥塞控制共同决定暴露通信和完成时间。

## Abstract
Mixture-of-experts (MoE) models expand capacity via sparse activation, but inference across GPUs introduces tensor-parallel (TP) collectives and expert-parallel (EP) dispatch and combine operations. Completion time depends not just on communication volume but on how logical groups map onto intra-server interconnects, GPU--NIC connections, and the inter-node network. Using ASTRA-sim with the NS-3 discrete-event backend, we build a controlled matrix of 32 GPU ranks with data and pipeline parallelism fixed at one. Workloads are fixed-length 4096-token prefill-like synthetic Chakra traces from four MoE configurations. Experiments cover six server topologies, four TP/EP partitions, two TP collective algorithms, and four network/congestion-control modes, yielding 768 deterministic simulations. In the 144-configuration feedback-enabled subset per model, exposed communication accounts for 89.9%--95.8% of mean completion time. TP16EP2 requires 3.68--4.35x the mean completion time of TP2EP16. With fixed rank mapping, ASTRA-sim Double Binary Tree (DBT) incurs 28.3%--83.2% more time than Ring. InfiniBand-like High Precision Congestion Control (HPCC) is ~0.9% lower than HPCC over RDMA over Converged Ethernet (RoCE), whereas RoCE with Data Center Quantized Congestion Notification (DCQCN) is 23.8%--35.7% slower than RoCE HPCC. Topology effects are conditional: Topology~6 leads at low TP degrees but loses its advantage at high TP degrees, and additional GPUs or NICs help only when rank mapping balances traffic across injection paths. Under uniform 32-way sharding, the largest checkpoint-weight shard is ~48.75 GB per rank, so all configurations meet a 64 GB per-accelerator weight-residency criterion. Within the evaluated workload and simulator semantics, server topology, parallelism, collective implementation, and congestion control jointly determine exposed communication and completion time.
