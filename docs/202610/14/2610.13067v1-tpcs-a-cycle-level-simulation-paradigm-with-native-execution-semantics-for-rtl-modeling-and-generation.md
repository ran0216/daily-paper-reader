## 摘要
领域特定架构与微架构设计的日益多样化，要求在早期设计阶段具备快速且准确的评估方法。然而，现有方法面临一个根本性权衡：高层仿真器速度快但保真度不足，而基于 RTL 的工具虽能提供准确性，却需付出显著的仿真开销。这一差距限制了高效的 pre-RTL 微架构探索。因此，我们提出事务剪枝周期仿真（Transaction-Pruned Cycle Simulation, TPCS），一种新颖的建模与仿真框架。它作为一种统一的中间抽象，涵盖微架构建模、周期精确仿真和可综合 RTL 实现。TPCS 将组合逻辑组织为基于事务的函数调用，并利用宿主 CPU 的控制流自然地剪枝不活跃逻辑，从而无需显式事件调度或信号跟踪。这使得周期精确仿真能够以显著提升的性能进行。TPCS 保持与 RTL 的结构对应关系，并支持自动生成可综合设计。TPCS 在 SMP 系统上相比并行 Verilator 实现 36×-42× 的加速，在加速器上相比单线程 Verilator 实现 3.4×-7.2× 的加速，并在所评估的 trace 上具有周期级波形一致性。生成的 RTL 在时序和面积上与手工实现相当，时钟周期的相对变化为 -4.7% 到 +2.6%，标准单元面积的相对变化为 -3.3% 到 +4.0%。通过弥合高层建模与 RTL 设计之间的抽象鸿沟，TPCS 实现了统一且敏捷的 pre-RTL 工作流，从而支持兼具高性能与实现保真度的高效微架构探索。

## Abstract
The growing diversity of domain-specific architectures and microarchitectural designs demands fast and accurate evaluation methods during early design stages. However, existing approaches suffer from a fundamental trade-off: high-level simulators offer speed but lack fidelity, while RTL-based tools provide accuracy at the cost of significant simulation overhead. This gap limits efficient pre-RTL microarchitecture exploration. Therefore, we propose Transaction-Pruned Cycle Simulation (TPCS), a novel modeling and simulation framework. It serves as a unified intermediate abstraction spanning microarchitecture modeling, cycle-accurate simulation, and synthesizable RTL implementation. TPCS organizes combinational logic as transaction-based function invocations and leverages host CPU control flow to naturally prune inactive logic, eliminating the need for explicit event scheduling or signal tracking. This enables cycle-accurate simulation with significantly improved performance. TPCS preserves structural correspondence with RTL and supports automatic generation of synthesizable designs. TPCS achieves 36$\times$-42$\times$ speedups over parallel Verilator on SMP systems and 3.4$\times$-7.2$\times$ over single-threaded Verilator on accelerators, with cycle-level waveform agreement on the evaluated traces. The generated RTL demonstrates comparable timing and area to handcrafted implementations, with relative changes of -4.7\% to +2.6\% in clock period and -3.3\% to +4.0\% in standard-cell area. By bridging the abstraction gap between high-level modeling and RTL design, TPCS enables a unified and agile pre-RTL workflow, allowing efficient microarchitecture exploration with both high performance and implementation fidelity.
